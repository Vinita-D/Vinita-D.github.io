Tracking heatwaves in space and time (Quasi-Lagrangian heatwaves)

Open-source code that follows heatwaves and blocks through space and time

How do you find a heatwave in data? The usual way is to pick a place and check when its daily maximum temperature stays above normal for several days. But heatwaves are not fixed to one place: they are large patches of hot air that move, grow and change shape (Luo et al., 2022). It is a useful tool I build , to detect and track weather patterns and here I am showing an example of detected record breaking heatwave (orange color in the figure below)and its associated atmopsheric blocking (in white)

This code follows each heatwave as one moving object, from its start to its end. It uses two different approches of tracking avaiable at: github.com/Vinita-D/Quasi_Lagrangian_heatwave_detection

The record-breaking 2021 heatwave in North America: the detected heatwave (orange) and the block, shown as a circle (white).
