Vinita Deshmukh

Atmospheric scientist | Weather and climate extremes | Machine learning for weather and climate (developing)

This is me: 🥗, in my Rêve shirt

Atmospheric scientist expertise in large-scale weather patterns, Lagrangian modelling (FLEXPART, LAGRANTO), climate and weather modelling (GCM, ICON), machine learning skill and tools.

  • Postdoc, University of Vienna (2025–2026): a Lagrangian framework (FLEXPART + ERA5) that traces where the heat in heatwaves comes from. Horizon Europe project Edu4ClimAte.
  • PhD, LMD, CNRS/ENS-PSL Paris (2021–2024): atmospheric blocking and temperature extremes. Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellow (EDIPI), research stays at Imperial College London and Uppsala University.
  • Developing machine learning skills: which atmospheric blocks bring heatwaves (scikit-learn), and open code to track heatwaves.
  • Workshops: co-organised the satellite workshop How to read a storm at the Vienna Klima Biennale, and the atmospheric transport modelling Autumn School of the Edu4ClimAte consortium.
  • Weather talks: started a weekly weather discussion group at the University of Vienna.

Outside research: tennis, hiking and dancing. My French (A2) and German (A1) are works in progress, just like my backhand.
