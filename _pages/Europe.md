Living in a country that is not your home comes with its challenges and its bliss. I like to focus
on the bliss: every new language and every new culture opens my mind a little more, every day.

**Paris** is one of the most cultural cities I know. My favourite district is **Le Marais**, with
its narrow streets and old mansions. There you find the Musée Carnavalet, the museum of the history
of Paris, set in two beautiful historic mansions and free to visit. The creative architecture and
art of France will always hold the highest place in my heart.

**Jazz nights.** At Cabaret Sauvage, a hidden gem of Paris, I found some of the finest local jazz,
played by local French artists, in a place whose acoustics amazed me. American and European jazz feel
different: finding the difference is half the fun. I enjoy jazz more than opera, and most of all
with a big band.
