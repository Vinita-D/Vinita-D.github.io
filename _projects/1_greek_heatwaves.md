This project addresses the ambiguous role of surface fluxes (the origin of heat and moisture in the atmosphere) during heatwave. In particular heatwaves over Grecce.  Using FLEXPART air-mass transport model and ERA5 data, I followed the airmass back in time from the region of heatwaves (1982 to 2025) and measured how much heat it picked up from the ground along the way. Two types appear: heatwaves heated mainly over North Africa, and heatwaves fed by
both Eastern Europe and North Africa under a Balkan block. More complex result and finiding will be published soon :) !

The animation follows the air on its way to Greece. The colour shows its moist static energy: its
total heat, including the heat stored in its moisture.
